【About the Village】
Pocapoca comes from the Japanese “ぽかぽか”, meaning warmth that comforts the heart. All sorts of warm stories happen every day in Pocapoca Story Village, and whenever she has a moment, Chief Poca writes them down. That's how this place began…

【About Poca】
Poca is a picture-book author and illustrator from Taiwan. Her name comes from the Japanese “ぽかぽか”, warmth that comforts the heart, which is also what she hopes her work will do: illustrations and stories with real warmth that give people the courage to keep going. Her style is playful and childlike, with ideas that stay with you. Her books include the four-volume Little Kappa series, Festival Party in the Animal Village and The Sleepy King's Dream. She also hosts the children's story podcast “Chief Poca's Story Time” and the YouTube channel “Pocapoca Story Village”, so more people can meet her work in different ways.
